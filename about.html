About Kanaa Construction

Kanaa Construction is led by Er. K. Akash, a civil engineer based in Kumbakonam. We take a house from the first sketch to the final handover, and we also work with owners who only need drawings, an estimate or advice.

Our name comes from the Tamil word for a dream. Our tagline says how we work: let's lay the foundation for the future. Plans, elevations and structural drawings come first, so decisions are made calmly at the table and not on site.

Whether you are building a first home or renovating the one you have, you get a clear plan, an itemised cost and an engineer you can call directly.

  • Plan, elevation and structural drawing
  • Itemised cost estimation before you start
  • Construction with regular site checks
  • Honest consulting on plot, design and budget
