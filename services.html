Our Work Process

Consultation

We meet, visit the plot and listen to what you need, how many people will live there and what you plan to spend.

Plan & Elevation

We draw the floor plan and the front elevation. You review them and we revise until you are happy.

Structural Drawing

The engineer prepares the structural drawings so the building is sound and the site team can work from them.

Cost Estimate

You receive an itemised estimate of materials and labour before you decide to go ahead.

Construction

Work moves from foundation through walls and roof to finishing, with regular site checks.

Handover

We walk you through the finished house, then hand over the keys.
